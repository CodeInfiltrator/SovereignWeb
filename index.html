Frequently Asked Questions

How do I record an expense using my voice?

Simply activate Siri or tap the speaking button in the app and say, "I spent 50,000 for food." Sovereign will automatically parse the amount, categorize it under 'Food', and log it to your expenses.

How do I undo a mistake?

If Sovereign misheard you or you made a mistake, you can simply say "Undo my last transaction" or "Undo that," and it will automatically revert your balance.

Is VoiceOver supported?

Yes! Sovereign was built as an accessibility-first application. Every single button, label, and list item is fully optimized for VoiceOver to ensure 100% blind and visually impaired accessibility.
